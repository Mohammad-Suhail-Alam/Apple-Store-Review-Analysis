 Central Tendency (Mean, Median, Mode)
The mean represents the average rating.
The median is the middle value and is preferred when the data is skewed.
The mode is the most frequently occurring rating.
Insight: If the mode is lower than the mean and median, it suggests many users gave low ratings, even though the average is higher.
Spread of Purchase Amount (Range & IQR)
Range shows the difference between the maximum and minimum purchase amounts.
IQR (Interquartile Range) represents the middle 50% of purchases, reducing the impact of extreme values.
Insight: A large range with a small IQR suggests that most purchases are within a specific range, but some extreme values (outliers) exist.
Variability in Likes (Variance & Standard Deviation)
Variance measures how much the likes differ from the mean.
Standard deviation (square root of variance) shows the spread of likes in the same units.
Insight: A high standard deviation means there are a few very popular reviews with many likes, while most reviews get fewer likes.
Correlation Between Likes and Ratings
A correlation coefficient (r) close to +1 means strong positive correlation.
Insight: If r = 0.84, it means higher-rated reviews tend to receive more likes, but the relationship is not perfect.
Distribution of Ratings
A negatively skewed distribution (left tail) means most ratings are high (4-5 stars).
Insight: Users are generally satisfied with the apps.
Hypothesis Testing (T-test: Instagram vs. WhatsApp Ratings)
The null hypothesis (H₀): Instagram and WhatsApp have the same average rating.
If p-value > 0.05, we fail to reject the null hypothesis (no significant difference).
If p-value < 0.05, we reject the null hypothesis (Instagram has significantly higher ratings).
Insight: If p = 0.7868, we fail to reject H₀, meaning Instagram's ratings are not significantly higher.
Central Limit Theorem (CLT) and Sampling Distribution
The CLT states that when we take many samples, their means follow a normal distribution, even if the original data is skewed.
Insight: The sampling distribution in our analysis is bell-shaped, confirming the CLT.
